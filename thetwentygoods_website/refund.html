Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Thank you for shopping with TheTwentyGoods. This policy explains when we consider returns and how approved refunds are handled.

1. Eligible returns

We consider returns only in the following circumstances:

  • The item received is defective; or
  • The item delivered is incorrect or does not match the order confirmed by TheTwentyGoods.

We do not accept change-of-mind returns or returns for reasons such as a change in preference, colour choice or ordering the wrong model, where the item supplied is correct and not defective. This does not limit any rights a customer may have under applicable law.

2. How to report an issue

Please contact us as soon as reasonably possible after receiving the item. Send your order details, a description of the problem and clear photographs or video where relevant. Contact us by WhatsApp on 071 367 7904 or email thetwentygoodslk@gmail.com. Please wait for our instructions before sending an item back or arranging a collection.

3. Inspection and resolution

We may ask to inspect the item and verify the reported issue. If the claim is approved, we will discuss an appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement (subject to availability) or a refund. If a replacement is unavailable or unsuitable, we will discuss the refund. Items should be returned with their accessories, packaging and proof of purchase where reasonably possible. Please do not continue using an item where doing so may worsen a defect or create a safety risk.

4. Refund method — important

All approved refunds will be issued to the same payment method or payment medium used by the customer to initiate the original payment. For example, a payment made by card through PayHere will be refunded through the applicable PayHere/card refund process to the original card; a bank transfer will be refunded to the originating bank account after verification; and a Cash on Delivery order paid in cash will be refunded through the same cash payment medium, with the practical arrangement confirmed with the customer. We will not substitute an unrelated payment method without a lawful or payment-provider-required reason.

5. Refund processing time

We will inform you when a refund is approved and initiated. The time for the funds to appear depends on the original payment method, bank, card issuer or payment provider. PayHere/card refunds are subject to PayHere's processing procedures. Bank transfer refunds are subject to bank processing times.

6. Return delivery costs

Where a return is approved because we supplied an incorrect item or the item is defective, contact us before arranging return delivery so we can confirm the return method and responsibility for reasonable return costs. Do not dispatch an item without our instructions.
