Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

TheTwentyGoods respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect personal information when you visit our website, contact us or place an order.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on your interaction with us, we may collect your name, telephone number, email address, delivery address, order details, messages you send us, payment confirmation or transaction reference, and information needed to arrange delivery or collection. Basic technical information such as browser or device information may also be processed by the website hosting provider.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and confirm orders.
  • To process payments and verify bank transfers.
  • To arrange delivery or collection and provide customer support.
  • To handle complaints, defective or incorrect item claims, returns and refunds.
  • To maintain business records, prevent fraud and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Payments and service providers

Where you pay through PayHere, your payment is handled by PayHere and the relevant payment networks or financial institutions under their own processes and policies. We do not intend to collect or store your full card number, CVV or online banking password. We may receive transaction status, reference and payment-related information needed to reconcile your order. We may share relevant delivery details with courier or logistics providers and relevant information with banks, payment providers or professional advisers when necessary to complete a transaction or meet legal obligations.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We disclose it only when reasonably necessary to provide our services, process a payment, deliver an order, protect our business and customers, or comply with a lawful request or legal obligation.

5. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for order fulfilment, customer support, business records and legal requirements.

6. Cookies and website hosting

Our website host may use essential technical processes, logs or cookies to provide and secure the website. We do not claim to use advertising or analytics tracking unless such tools are added and disclosed. Your browser may allow you to manage cookies.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have provided, request correction of inaccurate details, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request and may retain certain records where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

8. Third-party websites

Links to PayHere, WhatsApp or other third-party services may take you to websites governed by their own privacy policies. Please review those policies before providing information to them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will publish the latest version here with a revised date.
